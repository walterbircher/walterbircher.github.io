II. cureEDMD EDMD Conference Privacy Notice

1. Information We Collect and How We Use It

When you register for the cureEDMD Conference, cureEDMD collects the information you provide, which may include your name, contact information, professional or community affiliation, conference attendance and presentation preferences, dietary restrictions, and accessibility needs. We use this information to administer the conference, communicate with you about the event, coordinate meals and accommodations, evaluate presentation interest, verify registration where appropriate, and maintain the safety and integrity of the event.

Please do not provide medical or other sensitive personal information beyond what is reasonably necessary for the dietary or accessibility accommodations you request.

2. Service Providers and Conference Partners

cureEDMD may share information with service providers and conference partners as reasonably necessary to administer the conference, including providers that host registration information and venues or caterers assisting with dietary and accessibility accommodations. We ask such parties to use the information only for the purposes for which it is provided.

cureEDMD does not sell or rent conference registration information.

3. Attendee Directory

If you affirmatively consent during registration, cureEDMD may include your name, organization (if provided), and email address in an attendee directory made available to registered conference participants for networking and collaboration. Participation in the directory is optional and is not a condition of conference registration. You may change your preference by contacting cureEDMD.

4. Respecting Patient and Family Privacy

The conference brings together researchers, clinicians, industry representatives, patients, caregivers, and families. Attendees are expected to respect the privacy of others. Please do not disclose or publish another person’s personal medical information, family circumstances, or other sensitive personal information without that person’s permission.

Attendees may share scientific information and general conference content unless a presentation or session is identified as confidential or subject to other restrictions. Please do not post identifiable health information about, or identifiable photographs or recordings of, another attendee without that person’s permission.

5. Photography and Recording

Photography and video recording may occur during the conference. During registration, you may choose whether cureEDMD may intentionally photograph or record you and use identifiable photographs or recordings of you in its mission-related communications, including its website, social media, printed materials, presentations, fundraising communications, and promotion of future events.

If you do not consent, cureEDMD will make reasonable efforts not to photograph or record you intentionally and not to use identifiable images or recordings of you in its materials. Because photography may occur in common areas and during group activities, cureEDMD cannot guarantee that you will not appear incidentally in a background or crowd image. Attendees who do not consent should also identify themselves at conference check-in so staff can more readily honor their preference.

6. Future Communications

If you consent during registration, cureEDMD may use your contact information to send you information about future cureEDMD events, research opportunities, programs, and organizational updates. You may withdraw that consent or unsubscribe at any time. Your choice does not affect your ability to attend the conference.

7. Data Security and Retention

cureEDMD uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect registration information against unauthorized access, use, or disclosure.

cureEDMD retains registration information only for as long as reasonably necessary for conference administration, recordkeeping, and purposes to which you have separately consented. Dietary and accessibility information will be deleted or de-identified when it is no longer reasonably needed for conference administration. If you consent to future communications, cureEDMD may retain your contact information for that purpose until you unsubscribe or request deletion, subject to applicable legal or administrative retention requirements.

8. Questions and Requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Notice, wish to update your registration preferences or attendee-directory choice, withdraw consent to future communications, or request deletion of information that cureEDMD is not required to retain, please contact info@cureedmd.org.
