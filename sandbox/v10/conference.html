“Connecting Discovery, Care, and Therapeutic Innovation”

Join the cureEDMD community in downtown Chicago for the first-ever conference focused on Emery-Dreifuss muscular dystrophy. The conference is open to everyone: researchers, clinicians, biotech industry leaders, EDMD advocates, patients and families.

Dates & Location

April 2–4, 2027 · Chicago, Illinois

Northwestern Medicine has secured conference space at the Hyatt Centric Chicago Magnificent Mile in downtown Chicago. The conference is planned to begin Friday mid- to late afternoon and end Sunday by early afternoon. Final timing and venue details will be confirmed here.

Hotel & Travel

Book your room at the Hyatt Centric Chicago Magnificent Mile through the conference room block. A credit card is required to secure your reservation.

The Hyatt room reservation cutoff is March 2, 2027, including ADA-accessible rooms. Staying at the Hyatt is optional; attendees are welcome to choose another hotel.

Need to arrive early or stay longer? Stays outside the April 2–4, 2027 conference dates can be arranged with advance notice. Contact the conference organizers at info@cureEDMD.org in advance so they can coordinate with the hotel.

Find parking and transportation options and local visitor information in the Northwestern Medicine visitor guide.

Attend the Conference

There is no conference fee. Sign-up will open soon.

Please sign up by March 28, 2027. Accessibility, hotel, and meal requests have earlier deadlines.
