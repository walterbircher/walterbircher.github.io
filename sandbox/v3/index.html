Our Mission and Vision

Emery-Dreifuss muscular dystrophy (EDMD) is a rare genetic neuromuscular condition that affects the joints, muscles, and heart. The mission of cureEDMD is to foster collaboration between patients, families, researchers, and drug developers to enable effective treatments that improve the lifespan and quality of life for those affected by EDMD.
