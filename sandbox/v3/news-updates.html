August 2026

Steps made towards Global Genes partnership

Global Genes, a leading rare disease patient advocacy organization, in conjunction with the University of Notre Dame Institute for Rare Diseases, recently completed a Global Genes Research Acceleration Program action plan for cureEDMD. cureEDMD was selected for this program based on our mission. Global Genes and Notre Dame conducted background research surveys and interviewed top researchers, leading clinicians, and cureEDMD officers to produce a comprehensive final report recommending specific, concrete steps cureEDMD can take to further advance our mission. cureEDMD is extremely thankful to Global Genes, Notre Dame, and the expert participants for all of the effort put into this outstanding report.
