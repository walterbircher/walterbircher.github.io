Chicago · April 2–4, 2027

EDMD Conference

“Connecting Discovery, Care, and Therapeutic Innovation”

Join the cureEDMD community in downtown Chicago for the first-ever conference focused on Emery-Dreifuss muscular dystrophy. The conference is open to everyone: researchers, clinicians, biotech industry leaders, EDMD advocates, patients and families.

Registration (Coming Soon)

Dates & Location

April 2–4, 2027 · Chicago, Illinois

Northwestern Medicine has secured conference space at the Hyatt Centric Chicago Magnificent Mile in downtown Chicago. The conference is planned to begin Friday mid- to late afternoon and end Sunday by early afternoon. Final timing and venue details will be confirmed here.

Hotel & Travel

Learn more about the Hyatt Centric Chicago Magnificent Mile and its parking and transportation options. The conference room-block booking link will be posted here when arrangements are finalized. You can also find local visitor information through Northwestern Medicine visitor guide.

Agenda

Friday afternoon, April 2, 2027

  • 12:00–2:00 pm: Registration
  • 2:00–2:15 pm: Welcome and general introduction
  • 2:15–3:30 pm: Brief summary of current knowledge in EDMD
    • Brief overview of EDMD
    • Potential disease mechanisms
    • Patient perspectives
  • 3:30–4:00 pm: Break
  • 4:00–5:00 pm: Overview of state of the art current treatment approaches
  • 5:00–6:00 pm: Round table topic discussion
    • Including clinical care discussion with families
  • 6:00 pm: Dinner

Saturday, April 3, 2027

  • Breakfast on your own
  • 9:00–10:30 am: Current knowledge of disease mechanisms
    • Research presentations with a focus on insights relevant for clinical translation
  • 10:30–11:00 am: Break
  • 11:00 am–12:00 pm: New models to study EDMD
  • 12:00–1:00 pm: Lunch
  • 1:00–1:45 pm: Poster session
  • 2:00–3:30 pm: Lessons learned from therapeutic strategies for other muscle diseases
  • 3:30–4:00 pm: Break
  • 4:00–5:00 pm: Towards clinical studies: natural history studies, patient registries, clinical endpoints, data collection, and clinical registries
  • 5:00–6:00 pm: Reception and networking
  • 6:00 pm: Dinner

Sunday, April 4, 2027

  • Breakfast on your own
  • 9:00–10:30 am: Novel therapies for EDMD
  • 10:30–11:00 am: Break
  • 11:00–11:45 am: Next steps and future endeavors
    • Discussions of key lessons from the conference and steps towards translation and therapies
  • 12:00 pm: Departure
