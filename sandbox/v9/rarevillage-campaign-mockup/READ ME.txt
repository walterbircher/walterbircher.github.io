THE PROPOSED CHANGES
- Use the new cureEDMD website's logo, navy/coral/blue palette, Farro headings,
  Source Sans 3 body text, and rounded buttons.
- Keep text left aligned and give the campaign copy more breathing room.
- Add the family photo already used on the new homepage.
- Give the donation choices a clear panel with one-time and monthly options.
- Keep Rare Village's fiscal-sponsorship role and donor-support details visible.
- Retain the original campaign illustration, mission, appeal, and FAQ copy.

The one-time and monthly amount choices match the existing campaign.
The incomplete contact address in the original copy (info@cureEDMD) has been
completed to info@cureEDMD.org. Additional short headings organize the copy.

WHY CAMPAIGN ACCESS WOULD HELP
This shows the visual continuity we could aim for when someone clicks Donate
on the new cureEDMD website. Campaign-editor access would let us check which
branding, layout, image, and typography controls Rare Village's platform
provides, then apply the supported improvements. The exact layout is a proposal;
the live platform's customization options still need to be checked.

This is a local mockup. Continue opens a preview message only. No personal or
payment information is requested, no money is collected, and the live campaign
has not been changed. Website/email links still point to the real organizations.
